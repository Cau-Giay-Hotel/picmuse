FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no sign-in anywhere in PicMuse. Open it and start.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos are chosen through the Android photo picker and stay in the app's own storage on your device. PicMuse has no server to send them to.

Is anything locked behind a payment?

No. Every template and both style counts are open. There is no premium tier and no credit system.

Does the app show ads?

Yes, the released version is supported by ads. Ads never gate a template or a result.

What exactly do the results look like today?

This version shows the sample result that ships with each template, and labels it as such on screen. It does not render a new image from your own photo yet.

How do I delete what I made?

Open Profile ▸ Generations and remove items there, or use Settings ▸ Reset app data to clear likes, follows, recent photos, generations and your profile in one step.
