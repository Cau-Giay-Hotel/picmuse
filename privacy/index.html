Privacy Policy Terms of Use

PicMuse ("the App") is developed and published by CauGiay Hotel ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App and the third-party services it uses collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Photos you use in the App

PicMuse processes your photos entirely on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any server. The App has no backend server of its own.

  • You select a photo using Android's built-in Photo Picker, which lets you choose a picture without granting the App broad access to your entire media library.
  • The photo you select is copied into the App's private, sandboxed storage on your device so it can be displayed and combined with a template.
  • Results are saved to the App's local storage and, if you choose to save/export, to your device's Pictures folder (Pictures/PicMuse) using Android's standard MediaStore. This save happens directly on your device — it does not pass through any server we operate.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its private storage. Photos you exported to your gallery remain until you delete them yourself.

2. Profile information

If you set a display name or avatar in the App's profile screen, this information is stored only on your device (local app storage) and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Permissions the App requests, and what each is for

Permission What it is used for
Internet / network state / Wi-Fi state Used by the third-party SDKs listed in Section 4 (advertising, analytics, crash reporting, attribution). The App's own photo-editing logic makes no network requests and does not upload your photos.
Advertising ID (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION) Lets the advertising and attribution SDKs described in Section 4 identify your device for showing and measuring ads.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Lets the App show notifications, e.g. when processing a result is ready.
Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, legacy devices only, Android 9 and below) Lets the App save an exported result to your gallery on older Android versions. On modern Android, saving uses the Photo Picker/MediaStore APIs and no broad storage permission is requested.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise/approximate location, or full photo-library-read permissions.

4. Third-party services

PicMuse's release build includes the following third-party SDKs, which operate under their own privacy policies and may collect data such as your advertising ID, device identifiers, general device/app information, crash diagnostics, and usage events:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and mediation partners — advertising. Collects advertising identifiers and device data to show and measure ads. See Google's Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — shows the consent form required for users in the EEA/UK and manages your consent choices for personalized advertising.
  • Firebase Analytics — app usage and interaction events. See Firebase and Google Privacy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and diagnostic data to help us find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches non-personal configuration values used to control app behavior.
  • Adjust — install attribution measurement. See Adjust Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Facebook) Android SDK — app events and advertising identifiers for attribution/marketing measurement. See Meta Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok Business SDK — app events and advertising identifiers for attribution/marketing measurement. See TikTok Privacy Policy.

None of these services receive your photos. What they may receive is limited to device/advertising identifiers, app-usage events, and crash diagnostics, as described above. We do not sell your personal information. We do not control what these third parties do with data they collect independently under their own privacy policies — please review the links above.

5. Advertising ID and opt-out

The advertising and attribution SDKs above may use your device's advertising ID for measurement and personalized ads. You can reset or limit this at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads, depending on your Android version), including "Delete advertising ID" on Android 12+. You can also manage EEA/UK consent choices at any time from the consent prompt shown in the App.

6. Accounts

PicMuse does not require or offer account creation, sign-in, or sign-up. There is no server-side user ID associated with you.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Photos, results, and preferences you store in the App stay on your device until you delete them in-app or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device.
  • We do not operate a server that stores your personal data, so there is no developer-side account or database to request deletion from. For data the third-party SDKs in Section 4 may hold (e.g. advertising ID, crash logs, attribution events), use the opt-out/deletion mechanisms provided by each service, or contact us below and we will help you exercise those rights where we can.

8. Children's privacy

PicMuse is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will address it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because PicMuse processes and stores your photos locally rather than on our servers, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device (deleting content in-app, revoking permissions, resetting your advertising ID, or uninstalling). For anything that involves the third-party services in Section 4, contact us and we will assist.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
